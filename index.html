Nvidia's slide deepens to $149.30 as hot inflation data rattles Wall Street

1Password discloses a breach through a hijacked employee GitHub account

Password manager 1Password said Wednesday it began investigating on November 19 after an attacker compromised an employee's corporate GitHub account and used it to reach backend systems, copying customer records — names, email addresses, account-creation and last-sign-in timestamps, and public keys — for a subset of customers. The company says vaults, vault keys, recovery keys and its apps were unaffected, and it has found no evidence of account takeovers.

Takeaway The breach ran through developer tooling, not the vault itself — a reminder that a password manager's weakest point can be the GitHub account of whoever builds it.

TechCrunch →

OpenAI starts testing a merged ChatGPT and Codex usage allowance

OpenAI began testing a combined "Chat" and "Work" ChatGPT experience with a small group of users on November 25, ahead of a planned December rollout that would give Plus, Pro and Business subscribers one shared, token-metered usage allowance across ChatGPT and Codex instead of separate limits. OpenAI says most users won't notice more frequent resets, but its heaviest users may hit limits sooner.

Takeaway Pooling the meter instead of raising it is OpenAI managing compute scarcity by making power users choose between a long chat and a long coding session.

CISA's deadline passes for two more actively exploited flaws

November 24 was the deadline CISA set for US federal civilian agencies to patch an actively exploited Cisco BroadWorks flaw (CVE-2026-20320) and an Openwave Webtop Media Transcoder flaw (CVE-2026-111793), both added to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on November 10. The Cisco flaw can let an unauthenticated remote attacker read sensitive files; the Openwave flaw requires an authenticated user. Both vendors have shipped fixes.

Takeaway A second patch deadline in two weeks for actively exploited bugs is routine by now — the real news is how little attention routine security debt gets next to AI headlines.

BleepingComputer →

Ex-OpenAI engineer accused of planting hidden recorder gets a January trial date

A federal judge set a January 20, 2027 trial date for Abdulrahman Alfozan, the former OpenAI engineer charged with hiding an audio recording device inside a USB port in an executive conference room at OpenAI's San Francisco offices, at a November 17 hearing. The judge also ordered prosecutors to turn over all FBI reports by November 24. Alfozan, a Saudi national, has pleaded not guilty and remains detained without bail.

Takeaway A trial date two months out keeps the case, and the unanswered question of what the recordings were for, firmly open through the new year.

The Next Web →

Tesla's Texas robotaxi lead over Waymo widens to 186 vehicles

Tesla's Robotaxi LLC had 1,507 vehicles registered as automated vehicles with the Texas DMV as of November 18, after adding roughly 200 Cybercabs the day before, against 1,321 for Waymo — a lead of about 186 vehicles. The two companies have traded the top spot repeatedly through November since Tesla first edged ahead on November 10.

Takeaway Registering more Cybercabs with the state is a cheaper way to lead a headline than actually running more driverless trips.

Tata Sons' board meeting on its RBI-linked restructuring reportedly postponed

Tata Sons' board meeting, originally scheduled for today to take up Noel Tata's business-restructuring plan tied to the RBI's rejection of the company's bid to deregister as a core investment company, and Shapoorji Pallonji Group's request for about ₹25,000 crore in funding, has reportedly been postponed. Noel Tata is said to have objected to his proposed agenda items being placed sixth on the board's list and sought the full statutory notice period; no new meeting date has been announced.

Takeaway Postponing the meeting is itself a signal — it shows the Noel Tata camp can still stall the board's own calendar, not just dissent once it convenes.

Coal India's Mahanadi Coalfields readies a ₹8,981 crore listing for Friday

Mahanadi Coalfields, a Coal India subsidiary, priced its IPO in a ₹129-136 band for an offer-for-sale of about 66.18 crore shares — a 10% stake sold entirely by parent Coal India — raising roughly ₹8,981 crore. Bidding ran November 19-23, allotment was finalized November 25, and the stock is scheduled to list on the BSE and NSE on Friday, November 27.

Takeaway A parent company selling down a 10% stake in its own profitable subsidiary is Coal India raising cash without giving up control of it.

IPOWatch →

Ola Electric completes its shift from company stores to a dealer network

Ola Electric stopped taking orders through its own company-run stores on November 1, continuing a transition that began in September; by November 8 it had 255 dealer partners running 319 stores, with most company-owned outlets expected to close by December and a target of over 700 dealer stores by March 2027. The company says the move cuts fixed operating costs and improves working capital.

Takeaway Abandoning the direct-to-consumer model it was built on is Ola Electric's clearest admission that EV retail in India still needs a dealer's balance sheet.

Autocar India →

UPI processes a record 25.47 billion transactions in October

India's Unified Payments Interface handled 25.47 billion transactions worth more than ₹31.5 lakh crore in October, according to National Payments Corporation of India data, with daily transaction value crossing ₹1 lakh crore. PhonePe and Google Pay continue to process the large majority of that volume between them, though newer entrants have been slowly chipping into their combined share.

Takeaway UPI keeps setting volume records even as the two-app duopoly that built the network slowly loses ground — scale and concentration are no longer moving together.

Entrepreneur India →

Big Tech's AI capex estimate climbs toward $750 billion for 2026

Combined 2026 capital-spending guidance from Amazon, Microsoft, Alphabet and Meta has crept upward through the year, with independent analyst estimates now putting the four hyperscalers' AI-related capex near $720-750 billion, up from around $650 billion estimated in February. Microsoft has attributed roughly $25 billion of its own increase to rising memory-chip and component costs.

Takeaway The spending estimate keeps revising upward even as "is this a bubble" talk gets louder — confidence and discipline are moving in opposite directions.

Sensex and Nifty stage their sharpest rally in weeks, erasing most of Monday's rout

The BSE Sensex surged 1,502.29 points (1.82%) to 83,938.28 and the Nifty 50 jumped 469.60 points (1.85%) to 25,819.25 on Wednesday, reclaiming most of Monday's selloff after Tuesday's Guru Nanak Jayanti holiday. The rally was broad-based and led by Nifty FMCG, up 3.79%, with Hindustan Unilever the top Nifty gainer at 6.08%.

Takeaway A single day of buying erased most of Monday's panic — a reminder that broad indices often overreact to one bad session.

Fed funds futures now price a December rate hike, not a cut

Fed funds futures imply roughly 90% odds of a quarter-point rate hike at the Fed's December 9 meeting, with any size of cut priced at only about 2%, after hotter-than-expected PCE inflation data this week. Oxford Economics had already flagged in mid-November that it expected a December hike, cautioning markets were even overpricing the odds of a larger move.

Takeaway Markets no longer treating a cut as the base case is its own kind of tightening, even before the Fed actually does anything.

FIIs turn net buyers after a months-long selling streak

Foreign institutional investors bought roughly ₹1,01,640 crore of Indian equities in November through November 19 — ending six straight months of net selling from May to October — helped by Brent crude's drop to near $97 a barrel in early November. FIIs added a further net ₹5,672.99 crore on Wednesday's session alone.

Takeaway A multi-month foreign-selling streak has flipped into one of 2026's strongest buying stretches just as cheaper crude eases India's import math.
