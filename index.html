Nvidia gives back much of Monday's Huang-reinstatement rally, falling 5.86%

Nvidia shares closed down 5.86% at $155.38 on Tuesday, giving back a large share of Monday's 31% rally that followed Jensen Huang's reinstatement as CEO. The broader semiconductor index fell about 7.9% on the day, with the pullback coming alongside renewed Fed rate-hike bets and Huang's December 1-3 congressional testimony on export-control compliance still ahead.

Takeaway Giving back a third of a 31% one-day gain within 24 hours says the market isn't convinced the reinstatement actually settled anything.

Former OpenAI engineer pleads not guilty to planting hidden recording devices at its offices

Abdulrahman Alfozan, a Saudi national and former OpenAI engineer, pleaded not guilty on November 6 to a federal charge of intercepting oral communications. Prosecutors say he hid a recording device behind a TV in an executive conference room at OpenAI's San Francisco office, and a second device in an unattended bag; he has been held without bail since his October 9 arrest, and prosecutors allege he had Saudi government backing, which he denies.

Takeaway A hidden device behind a TV in a room he wasn't authorized to enter is a reminder that an AI lab's biggest security risk isn't always digital.

CISA's deadline passes for an actively exploited Outlook flaw that steals password hashes

November 24 was the deadline CISA set for US federal civilian agencies to patch CVE-2026-124194, an actively exploited Outlook vulnerability that can leak a user's NTLMv2 password hash when they open a crafted file. CISA added the flaw to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on November 10.

Takeaway A bug that leaks password hashes just from opening a file is the kind of basic risk that all the AI headlines tend to crowd out.

Tesla's robotaxi fleet edges past Waymo's in Texas registrations

As of November 10, Tesla's Robotaxi LLC had 1,302 vehicles registered in Texas against Waymo's 1,297 — a razor-thin lead driven by new Model Y and Cybercab additions. Waymo still holds a roughly tenfold lead in actual US weekly mileage nationwide.

Takeaway Leading a state vehicle registry by five cars while trailing tenfold on actual miles driven is a fleet-count headline standing in for the race that actually matters.

Tata Sons heads into Thursday's board meeting with two big asks still unresolved

Noel Tata has asked Tata Sons' board to take up, at its November 26 meeting, a restructuring tied to the RBI's listing directive for upper-layer NBFCs and a ₹25,000 crore funding request from the Shapoorji Pallonji Group seeking partial stake monetisation. The RBI reiterated on November 16 that Tata Sons must comply with listing rules; Tata Trusts opposes a listing.

Takeaway Two unrelated asks landing on the same agenda is Noel Tata betting the board can't keep deferring either one forever.

India's startups raise about $195 million this week, a second straight weekly rise

Indian startups raised roughly $195 million across 24 deals this week, per Inc42 — up about 14% from the prior week's $171 million, marking back-to-back weekly gains after a stretch of thin funding.

Takeaway Two decent weeks in a row is a real trend only if a third one follows — one bounce after a slump proves nothing by itself.

Swiggy's Instamart posts a wider loss even as revenue grows 33%

Swiggy's quick-commerce arm Instamart reported its adjusted EBITDA loss widening to ₹895 crore in its latest quarter even as operating revenue rose 33% year-on-year to ₹1,306 crore; the company says it believes Instamart's losses peaked this quarter as it keeps investing in customer acquisition and store expansion.

Takeaway Calling this quarter the peak loss is a claim investors will only believe once the next quarter's numbers actually confirm it.

Coal India's Mahanadi Coalfields readies a ₹9,500 crore IPO listing

Mahanadi Coalfields, a Coal India subsidiary, opened its ₹9,500 crore IPO — an offer-for-sale of 66.18 crore shares, a 10% stake, by parent Coal India — on November 19, with listing scheduled for November 27.

Takeaway A parent company selling down a 10% stake in its own subsidiary is Coal India raising cash without giving up control of it.

Big Tech's AI-spending binge keeps testing investor patience

Amazon, Microsoft, Google and Meta are on track to spend more than $720 billion this year on data centers and other AI-related capital expenditure. Meta's shares fell 7% after disclosing plans to raise up to $95 billion in new stock to help fund the buildout, and Alphabet posted negative free cash flow for a second straight quarter.

Takeaway Issuing new stock to fund a spending plan is a company telling you its own cash generation can't keep up with its own ambition.

Fed funds futures now price a December hike, not a cut

Fed funds futures are pricing roughly a 90% chance of a quarter-point rate hike at the Fed's December 9 meeting, with a cut of any size priced at only about 2%, extending the hawkish repricing that's followed Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's recent inflation warnings.
