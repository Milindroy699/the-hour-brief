AI & Tech

Apple posts a clean 12% revenue beat in CEO John Ternus's first earnings call as OpenAI reportedly moves to buy Google's TPU chips to diversify beyond Nvidia, even as Google DeepMind's Genie 4 adds native audio to real-time AI-generated worlds, a trojanized Claude Code extension plants an SSH backdoor on Cursor's marketplace, Apple hands Siri in China to Alibaba's Qwen, and a prominent Wall Street bull sells most of his Nvidia stake over AI-spending worries

Takeaway Today’s AI story is quiet risk management everywhere at once — OpenAI hedging its chip supply, Apple outsourcing Siri’s brain in China, and a prominent bull trimming Nvidia all point to an industry managing its own concentration risk rather than racing blindly ahead.

Apple's first earnings call under CEO John Ternus beats estimates with a 12% revenue jump

Apple reported fiscal Q4 2026 results on November 2 — the quarter ended September 26 and John Ternus's first earnings call as CEO: revenue of $114.5 billion, up 12% year-over-year and a September-quarter record, beating consensus of about $113.3 billion. Diluted EPS came in at $2.10, up 14%, including a $0.05 benefit from tariff refunds; gross margin was 47.9%. Services revenue hit an all-time high of $31.6 billion, up 10%. Full fiscal-year 2026 revenue reached $478.8 billion, up 15%, with record EPS of $8.98, up 20%.

Takeaway A clean beat gives Ternus a strong first outing, but the real test — sustaining Services growth and December-quarter execution without Tim Cook — is still ahead.

OpenAI reportedly plans to buy Google's TPU chips to diversify beyond Nvidia

The Information reported around October 27-28 that OpenAI plans to purchase — not just rent — Google's TPU chips for the first time, adding a second hardware supplier alongside Nvidia. OpenAI has rented Google TPU capacity since June 2025; this would mark a step further into ownership, according to Techstrong.ai. Price, volume and delivery timelines are undisclosed. OpenAI remains among Nvidia's largest customers and is separately developing its own "Jalapeño" chip with Broadcom.

Takeaway OpenAI hedging its single biggest cost center across two rival chipmakers shows how little pricing leverage Nvidia's dominance actually buys it once a customer gets big enough.

Techstrong.ai →

Google DeepMind unveils Genie 4, a real-time world model with native audio

Google DeepMind announced Genie 4 on October 27 as a research preview for select testers: it generates interactive, real-time 3D worlds and, new versus Genie 3, native audio — music, ambient sound, dialogue — alongside visuals, steerable via text prompts for camera moves, weather and new characters. Gemini models handle the underlying reasoning. Rollout is staged to Google AI Ultra subscribers via Project Genie plus early API and Studio partners, with no pricing yet; DeepMind acknowledges scene details can still drift over longer simulations.

Takeaway Adding audio to a generative world model is a deliberate push toward simulations usable for robotics and game training, not just visual demos.

A trojanized Claude Code extension plants an SSH backdoor via Cursor's marketplace

Cursor disclosed that a malicious version (2.1.313, live roughly 9:27am–8:35pm UTC on October 29) of the Anthropic Claude Code extension was distributed through its marketplace, installing a persistent SSH backdoor. The payload collects machine name, user, OS, home folder, git-configured email, public IP and the names of up to 40 environment variables, according to BankInfoSecurity. Cursor published remediation scripts and indicators of compromise; researchers recommend isolating and forensically examining any affected machine, since removing the extension alone doesn't remove the backdoor.

Takeaway A trojanized build slipping through a major AI-coding marketplace for nearly 11 hours shows how thin the vetting layer still is on tools millions of developers now trust by default.

Apple brings Alibaba's Qwen into Siri for mainland China, developer testing starts November 4

Apple and Alibaba announced on October 31 that Apple Intelligence is coming to China, powered by Alibaba's Qwen model rather than Apple's own, according to TechNode. Developer beta testing begins November 4; public rollout follows via iOS 27.3 in December, about two years after Apple Intelligence launched in the US. Qwen will power Siri, Writing Tools and Visual Intelligence for eligible users; the newer agentic Siri shown at WWDC 2026 is excluded from this first wave.

Takeaway Apple outsourcing Siri's brain to a Chinese rival's model, rather than building its own for the market, is a rare admission that in China it can't out-build local AI players.

TechNode →

A Wall Street veteran sells most of his Nvidia stake, warns AI infrastructure spending may be peaking

Steve Weiss, founder of Short Hills Capital Partners, sold most of his Nvidia holdings around November 2, telling CNBC that while hyperscalers still post strong growth, he doubts they can sustain the current pace of AI capital spending, according to Benzinga. He flagged over-ordering of memory chips, semiconductors and data-center capacity, calling AI investment "far from over" but expecting capex growth to slow as companies scrutinize returns. Nvidia shares had already fallen about 4.2% on October 30 in an Intel-guidance-led chip selloff; its next earnings report is due November 17.

Takeaway A prominent bull trimming Nvidia specifically over capex sustainability, not demand, signals the AI-spending debate is shifting from "is the technology real" to "can anyone afford to keep funding it."

Benzinga →

AI code-testing startup Blacksmith hits a $1 billion valuation on a Sequoia-led Series C

Blacksmith, which builds AI-era CI/testing infrastructure, raised $66 million at a $1 billion valuation — roughly a 10x jump from its $550 million mark two months earlier after a $45 million Peak XV-led Series B, according to AI Weekly. The company says its ARR grew 17x since January 2026, reported around October 22.

Takeaway The pace of AI-written code is now visibly straining CI and testing infrastructure enough to mint a unicorn in months, not years.

Product & Business

Affle's $420 million all-cash bid for Nasdaq-listed Perion marks a rare Indian outbound tech acquisition as Adani Ports raises its full-year guidance on an 18% profit jump and Nestlé India's profit surges 36%, even as Sun Pharma's profit falls sharply despite double-digit revenue growth, SAIL's profit more than quadruples on a low base, Bengaluru's Clinics On Cloud raises ₹120 crore for global expansion, and Shopify and Pfizer both report third-quarter results today

Takeaway Today’s business story is India’s earnings season cutting both ways — big profit jumps at Adani Ports, Nestlé and SAIL look strong on paper, but Sun Pharma’s margin squeeze and Affle buying outward rather than being bought show the picture isn’t uniformly rosy.

Affle agrees to pay $420 million in cash for Nasdaq-listed Israeli adtech firm Perion

India-listed adtech firm Affle agreed on November 1 to acquire Perion Network in an all-cash deal at $11.70 a share, an equity value of about $420 million — a 34.5% premium to Perion's October 30 close, according to SEC Form 6-K filings. The deal is financed via debt from Citibank and isn't subject to a financing condition; Perion has withdrawn its FY26 guidance and suspended its buyback.

Takeaway An Indian adtech firm buying a Nasdaq-listed Israeli rival for $420 million in cash is a notable outbound-M&A moment for Indian tech, not the usual direction of capital flow.

Adani Ports raises its full-year guidance after Q2 profit rises about 18%

Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone's board approved Q2 FY27 results on October 28: net profit rose roughly 18% year-over-year to around ₹3,680 crore, while consolidated revenue climbed 24% to ₹11,340 crore and EBITDA grew 23% to ₹6,807 crore, according to Business Standard, Kotak Neo and PSU Connect. The company raised its FY27 revenue and EBITDA guidance by ₹1,000 crore each on the back of the quarter.

Takeaway Raising full-year guidance mid-year, not just beating the quarter, is Adani Ports signaling it expects this cargo-volume strength to hold rather than fade.

Nestlé India's profit jumps 36% as revenue grows over 20%

Nestlé India's standalone Q2 FY27 results, announced October 15, showed profit of ₹1,026.77 crore, up 36.3% year-over-year, on revenue up about 20.3%, according to Free Press Journal. The company attributed the profit gain largely to a rise in operating profit.

Takeaway A 36% profit jump on a 20% revenue gain means Nestlé India's margins, not just its top line, are doing real work this quarter.

Sun Pharma's profit falls about 36% even as revenue grows nearly 12%

Sun Pharmaceutical Industries' Q2 FY27 results, declared October 30, showed revenue of ₹16,198.71 crore, up 11.9% year-over-year, while net profit fell to roughly ₹1,988.53 crore, down about 36% from the year-ago quarter, according to Univest and Investywise. The profit figure came in well below pre-results analyst consensus of ₹3,085–3,118 crore.

Takeaway Double-digit revenue growth doing nothing to stop a sharp profit decline is the kind of divergence that tends to draw more scrutiny than either number alone.

SAIL's profit surges 315% to ₹1,774 crore on a low base

Steel Authority of India's Q2 FY27 standalone net profit jumped 315% year-over-year to ₹1,774 crore, according to a release dated October 27 covered by PSU Connect. The sharp percentage gain reflects a weak year-ago quarter as much as the current one's strength.

Takeaway A 315% jump reads dramatically, but it says more about how low last year's base was than about SAIL suddenly having a breakout quarter.

PSU Connect →

Bengaluru's Clinics On Cloud raises ₹120 crore to expand its diagnostics-screening chain abroad

Dubai-based Model42 led a ₹120 crore Series A (about $14.26 million, at a roughly ₹600 crore valuation) for Clinics On Cloud, the India-founded "Health ATM" diagnostics-screening chain started in 2018 by Abhay and Manashrri Agarwal, according to DigitalHealthNews.com. The company has 3,500-plus installations and more than 12 million screenings across eight-plus countries; the funds go toward UAE, Africa, US and Southeast Asia expansion and AI diagnostics, reported around October 26-28.

Takeaway A Gulf healthcare investor backing an Indian diagnostics-kiosk chain's global expansion shows Indian health-tech increasingly selling outward, not just scaling domestically.

Shopify and Pfizer both report third-quarter results today, before the US market open

Shopify reports Q3 2026 results before Tuesday's open, with guidance pointing to revenue of $3.73–3.78 billion versus Q2's 34% growth to $3.58 billion; Pfizer also reports before the open, with consensus around $15.86 billion in revenue and adjusted EPS near $0.72–0.73, after beating estimates in each of its last eight quarters, according to SEC filings and Benzinga's earnings-forecast pages.

Takeaway Two very different growth stories — a platform still guiding to 30%-plus growth and a pharma giant defending a multi-year beat streak — report on the same morning, a useful read on how differently "beating expectations" gets defined by sector.

Sensex and Nifty rally for a third straight session as tumbling crude lifts oil-and-gas and metal stocks

The Sensex closed Monday, November 2, at 75,692.78, up 1,233.25 points or 1.66%, while the Nifty 50 rose 387.70 points, or 1.68%, to 23,440.80, their third straight gain, according to Business Standard and Kotak Neo. Nifty Oil & Gas and Nifty Metal led the advance as crude prices tumbled, while Nifty IT and Nifty Media lagged the broader rally.

Takeaway A rally led by oil-sensitive sectors, not banks or IT, shows just how directly falling crude is feeding straight into Dalal Street's mood right now.

Business Standard →

The S&P 500 falls for a third straight session despite a sharp drop in oil prices

The S&P 500 fell 0.51% to 7,541.40 on Monday, November 2, its third straight loss, even as the Dow Jones Industrial Average added 0.4% to 51,983.98 and the Nasdaq Composite dropped 1.1%. Nvidia swung from an early gain to a loss and helped drag the index lower as tech and AI stocks sold off, with the pullback overriding both a sharp slide in crude prices and a stronger-than-expected ISM manufacturing reading.

Takeaway A session where falling oil and a good economic print both failed to lift the market shows how much a handful of AI-linked stocks are now dictating Wall Street's direction on any given day.

Apple's clean Q4 beat lands after Monday's close, hours before Tuesday's Indian open

Apple reported fiscal Q4 2026 results after Monday's US close, beating revenue and EPS estimates with 12% revenue growth and its first earnings call under CEO John Ternus. Because the report landed after Indian markets had already shut for Monday, any reaction in IT and supply-chain-linked stocks lands only in Tuesday's session.

Takeaway India's market gets its first real chance to price in Apple's results only today — Monday's rally happened without this information in hand.

Brent crude tumbles about 5% to roughly $108 a barrel

Brent crude fell about 5% on Monday, November 2, to $107.76 a barrel, according to Trading Economics' price tracker, a sharp pullback from the multi-month highs near $115 seen in late October. The move extended a retreat already under way over the prior several sessions.

Takeaway A 5% single-day move in crude is itself the story — it's the kind of swing that reshapes India's oil-import bill and rupee outlook well before any single cause gets fully sorted out.

The NSE's rare Sunday Muhurat-trading session is now five days away

NSE and BSE's special Diwali Muhurat trading session, held on a Sunday this year for Laxmi Pujan, falls on November 8, with trading running roughly 2:20pm–3:30pm IST and all trades settling normally, according to an NSE circular dated October 19 and reported by Outlook Money. The following Tuesday, November 10, is a separate scheduled market holiday for Diwali-Balipratipada.

Takeaway A symbolic one-hour session won't move much on its own, but it's a reminder that this week's regular trading days are compressed around it.
