AI & Tech

Nvidia's $500B bet meets an AI price war

Nvidia guarantees a quarter of its own chips' resale value in a $500B financing push

Nvidia has lined up Apollo, Blackstone, BlackRock, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs and KKR to funnel more than $500 billion in third-party capital into AI data centers — and is personally backstopping up to 25% of the residual value of its GPUs in individual deals. If an operator can't repay debt and a GPU fire-sale falls short, Nvidia eats up to a quarter of the gap, an unusual attempt to manufacture a secondary market for aging chips.

IBM goes all-in on OpenAI for enterprise AI delivery

IBM announced a strategic partnership on 13 August embedding GPT-5.6, Codex and ChatGPT Work into IBM Consulting Advantage, standing up a dedicated "OpenAI Practice" staffed with thousands of OpenAI-certified consultants across financial services, government, telecom and retail. The deal also folds in cybersecurity collaboration via OpenAI's Daybreak Cyber Partner Program.

DeepSeek hikes API prices by up to 1,100% as demand outstrips capacity

Effective 16 August, DeepSeek is raising V4-Flash and V4-Pro API prices sharply: V4-Flash peak-hour input tokens jump from $0.14 to $0.44 per million, while V4-Pro peak pricing rises from $0.435 to $1.32 per million. The new peak/off-peak tiered pricing is designed to push developer workloads into less congested hours — a rare price hike in a market otherwise defined by cuts.

OpenAI ships "Ultrafast" mode, runs GPT-5.6 Sol at 14x normal speed

OpenAI's new inference mode delivers up to 750 output tokens per second — roughly 14 times the standard rate for its flagship model. The launch is aimed squarely at latency-sensitive agentic and coding workloads, another sign the model race is shifting from raw capability toward speed and cost as the competitive lever.

Apple trains its own AI model for China, with Alibaba's help

Apple has built a China-specific large language model with assistance from Alibaba, reportedly the first foreign company approved by Beijing to offer a proprietary AI model domestically. The model runs alongside Apple's existing partner systems as it prepares to launch Apple Intelligence in China following Cyberspace Administration registration completed last month.

Microsoft starts merging consumer and business Copilot into one "super app"

Microsoft has begun combining consumer Copilot and Microsoft 365 Copilot into a single app that lets users switch between personal and work accounts without data crossing over — mobile and web first, Windows and Mac following in September. In the process it's retiring underused consumer features, including Group Chats, AI podcast generation, Copilot Labs and Deep Research, by 18 August.

Anthropic's Q2 revenue surges past $11.5B ahead of a fall IPO

Anthropic told investors its preliminary Q2 revenue topped $11.5 billion — more than 14 times the $787 million logged a year earlier and up from $4.73 billion in Q1 — with positive adjusted operating income. Investors are reportedly targeting an October IPO at a valuation north of $2 trillion, which would make it the largest listing in history.

Google's Gemini app crosses 1 billion monthly users

Google says the Gemini app passed 1 billion monthly active users this month — its fastest-growing product ever, climbing from 400 million in May 2025 to 900 million by May 2026, then adding another 100 million-plus in under a month, driven by integration across Android, Chrome, Search and Workspace. ChatGPT reportedly reached 1 billion users first, in June, but growth there is said to be slowing as Gemini closes the gap.

Everyone's doubling down — on data centers and DRHPs

L&T bags a $1.57B order to build India's largest AI data centre

Larsen & Toubro's AI infrastructure arm, LTN Compute, secured an order worth ₹10,000–15,000 crore ($1.05–1.57B) from US cloud platform Together AI on 13 August — L&T's first major move into the "AI factory" business. The facility, at L&T's Vyoma.AI campus in Chennai, will run 10,000 Nvidia B300 GPUs, India's largest single-cluster AI deployment so far.

Business Standard →

Zetwerk files updated IPO papers for ₹2,600 crore

Manufacturing platform Zetwerk refiled its DRHP with SEBI on 14 August, confirming a ₹2,600 crore fresh issue plus an offer-for-sale from promoters and investors including Peak XV, Accel and Lightspeed. FY26 revenue jumped 40.4% to ₹15,913 crore and its order book doubled to ₹12,370 crore — but 69% of the fresh raise goes straight to debt repayment, not growth capital.

Table Space files DRHP for an ₹800 crore managed-workspace IPO

Bengaluru-based Table Space, which leases 9.33 million sq ft to GCCs and Fortune 500 tenants across 33 clusters, filed for an IPO comprising a ₹800 crore fresh issue plus an OFS of up to 65.48 million shares on 11 August — the latest coworking/managed-office operator to test public markets, betting India's GCC office boom still has runway.

Maharashtra FDA suspends food licences at Blinkit, Zepto and Instamart dark stores

Following an 7 August inspection at a Blinkit Malad West store that found cockroaches on produce, expired stock and food stored on the floor, Maharashtra's FDA launched a state-wide sweep of 86 quick-commerce facilities and suspended licences at 14 outlets across Blinkit, Zepto and Swiggy Instamart on 13 August — the sharpest regulatory jolt yet to the quick-commerce "dark store" model.

Fed September rate-hike odds tumble after a shock July jobs miss

Nonfarm payrolls fell by roughly 23,000 in July versus a Wall Street estimate near +85,000, sharply cutting the odds markets had assigned to a Fed rate hike — not cut — at the September meeting; a benign July CPI print days later reinforced the shift. The unusual backdrop of the Fed weighing whether to tighten, rather than ease, is shaping global risk appetite right now.
