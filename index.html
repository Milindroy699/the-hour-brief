AI & Tech

CISA sets a December 2 deadline for a newly flagged, actively exploited flaw in the open-source LiteLLM AI gateway, as Microsoft's own November 12 global outage that knocked out Outlook, Teams and Copilot for hours turns out to trace back to an earlier network-capacity upgrade rather than an attack, even as Google rolls a standalone Gemini app out to Apple CarPlay worldwide, Nectar launches an AI "Creator Agent" built by two former Meta employees to automate influencer marketing, and AMD's record third quarter puts it on pace to out-earn Intel for the first time

Takeaway Today’s AI story splits between infrastructure fragility and infrastructure ambition — a patch deadline and a major outage both trace back to ordinary engineering debt, while Google, Nectar and AMD keep building out the compute and tools layer regardless.

CISA sets a December 2 deadline for an actively exploited flaw in the LiteLLM AI gateway

CISA added four actively exploited vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on November 18 — flaws in Synology DSM, the open-source LiteLLM AI gateway, Apache Axis 1.x, and Vanderbilt REDCap. Federal civilian agencies faced an emergency three-day deadline of November 21 to patch the Synology, Axis and REDCap flaws; the LiteLLM flaw carries a longer deadline of December 2. No patch exists yet for the Apache Axis 1.x flaw, which reached end-of-life years ago.

Takeaway An AI-specific tool landing on the same list as decade-old, patchless software is a reminder that the AI stack inherits all of security's old problems, not just new ones.

Microsoft's global 365 outage traced to its own earlier network-capacity upgrade

A cascading Exchange Online failure on November 12 took down Outlook, Teams, SharePoint and Copilot worldwide, with Downdetector logging more than 130,000 Outlook problem reports at its peak. Microsoft's preliminary post-incident report, published November 15, attributes the outage to factors introduced during an earlier network-capacity upgrade rather than any attack, and the company says it found no evidence of malicious activity.

Takeaway A routine capacity upgrade taking down email, chat and Copilot at once shows how tightly Microsoft has now wired its AI assistant into the same infrastructure as everything else.

Google's Gemini app lands as a standalone option on Apple CarPlay worldwide

Google began rolling out a dedicated Gemini app for Apple CarPlay globally in mid-November, letting drivers start a Gemini Live voice conversation that carries over to the car's display. The feature requires the latest version of the Gemini app and having opened Gemini Live at least once on the iPhone first.

Takeaway Google getting Gemini onto CarPlay's home screen — Apple's own turf — is a small but direct shot at Siri's remaining advantage: being the voice assistant already sitting in the car.

Nectar launches an AI "Creator Agent" to automate influencer marketing

Nectar, the marketing platform founded by former Meta employees Misbah and Farah Uraizee, launched a "Creator Agent" on November 12 that searches more than 300 million creator profiles on Instagram and TikTok, drafts outreach in a brand's own voice, and tracks past partnerships, built on the company's Ren AI technology. Nectar says the agent will eventually be reachable through its own MCP, so brand teams could run the same workflow from Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini.

Takeaway Automating the outreach and vetting grunt work is Nectar betting that influencer marketing's real bottleneck was never finding creators — it was the hours spent messaging them.

AMD's record Q3 puts it on pace to out-earn Intel for the first time

AMD reported record Q3 2026 revenue of $13.4 billion, up 45% year-over-year, with data-center revenue nearly doubling to $8.5 billion, and guided to roughly $17 billion for Q4. Intel's Q3 revenue grew 21% to $16.5 billion with data-center revenue up 78% to $7.3 billion, but its Q4 guidance of $15-16 billion sits below even the low end of AMD's — which would make next quarter the first time AMD's guided revenue could exceed Intel's.

Takeaway Intel still has the bigger number today, but it's AMD's growth rate and guidance that are actually setting the pace in AI-era chip demand.

Product & Business

Mahanadi Coalfields lists on the NSE and BSE today after Coal India's ₹8,981 crore stake sale clears a sluggish opening day, as ratings agency ICRA projects India's Q2 FY27 GDP growth easing to a five-quarter low of 7.5% ahead of the government's November 30 release, even as Paytm wins the Reserve Bank's in-principle nod to restart its wallet business and India's October unemployment rate eases to an eight-month low of 4.9%

Takeaway Today’s business story is partial good news everywhere — a weak-starting IPO that still clears, a slowing growth estimate that's still strong, and a wallet business and a jobs number both easing back toward normal rather than breaking through to it.

Mahanadi Coalfields lists today after a soft start to its IPO

Mahanadi Coalfields, the Coal India subsidiary whose ₹8,981 crore IPO was entirely an offer-for-sale of a 10% stake by its parent, lists on the NSE and BSE today following a special pre-open auction from 9:00 to 9:45am IST to set the listing price. The issue priced at the top of its ₹129-136 band but drew weak Day 1 demand — bids for just 18.69 crore of the 49.31 crore shares on offer, or 0.38 times — before overall subscription recovered by the close of bidding on November 23.

Takeaway Pricing at the top of the band despite a weak opening day is Coal India betting that later-stage institutional demand would cover for retail's lukewarm response — and for now, it has.

ICRA projects India's Q2 growth easing to a five-quarter low of 7.5%

Ratings agency ICRA expects India's GDP growth for the July-September quarter to ease to a five-quarter low of 7.5%, citing external headwinds and a sub-par monsoon, ahead of the government's official release on November 30. That estimate is still above the RBI's own 7.2% projection, though below the Finance Ministry's monthly review figure of 7.35% and well off Q1 FY27's 7.8%.

Takeaway Every forecaster's number is different, but they all agree on the direction — growth is cooling from Q1's pace, just not by enough to actually worry anyone yet.

Paytm wins RBI's in-principle nod to restart its wallet business

The Reserve Bank of India gave Paytm's payments subsidiary, Paytm Payments Services Ltd, in-principle approval in a November 13 letter to restart its digital wallet business under a fresh prepaid payment instrument licence. The subsidiary still needs to complete prescribed system and cybersecurity audits and obtain final authorisation before it can actually issue wallets again; Paytm's stock touched a fresh 52-week high after the news broke.

Takeaway An "in-principle" nod is real progress but not a finish line — Paytm still has to pass the same kind of audits that got its banking arm shut down in the first place.

India's unemployment rate eases to an eight-month low of 4.9%

India's unemployment rate fell to 4.9% in October from 5.0% in September, below the 5.1% median forecast and the lowest reading in eight months, according to the National Statistics Office's monthly Periodic Labour Force Survey. Urban unemployment eased to 6.7% from 6.9% while rural unemployment held steady at 4.2%, and overall labour force participation rose to 55.7%.

Takeaway A labour market improving steadily in the background, away from the GDP and markets headlines, is the quieter half of India's growth story this month.

Sensex and Nifty close higher for a second straight session

The BSE Sensex rose 390.31 points (0.46%) to 84,328.59 and the Nifty 50 gained 65.25 points (0.25%) to 25,884.50 on Thursday, extending Wednesday's rally into a second straight day of gains. Nifty IT led sector gains, up 3.66%, followed by Nifty Realty, up 3.27%.

Takeaway Two sector-led up days in a row is a steadier recovery than Wednesday's single broad-based bounce — this time specific sectors, not the whole market, are doing the pulling.

Mahanadi Coalfields' listing-day auction sets today's opening trade

Mahanadi Coalfields moves from IPO to listed stock today, with a special pre-open session from 9:00 to 9:45am IST discovering the listing price before normal trading begins at 10am on the NSE and BSE. India's Q2 GDP release, due Monday, is the next big number markets are watching

The government is scheduled to release official July-September GDP figures on November 30, with forecasts ranging from ICRA's 7.5% to the RBI's 7.2% and SBI Ecowrap's more optimistic 7.8%. The spread itself signals genuine uncertainty about how much the quarter actually slowed from Q1 FY27's 7.8%.

Takeaway When forecasters disagree by close to a full percentage point days before the actual number, markets are flying into Monday's release mostly blind — expect the reaction to be as much about surprise as about the number itself.

Wall Street sits out Thanksgiving as Indian markets trade through it

US markets were closed Thursday, November 26, for the Thanksgiving holiday and are scheduled for an early 1pm ET close on Friday, leaving Wednesday's levels — the S&P 500 at 7,182.78 and the Nasdaq Composite at 23,585.80 — as the most recent read on Wall Street sentiment. Indian markets, which don't observe the US holiday, traded a full session regardless.

Takeaway A day with no fresh Wall Street data is a day Dalal Street effectively trades on its own momentum — which is exactly what Thursday's sector-led gains looked like.
