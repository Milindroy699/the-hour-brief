Before the news

There’s a Scandinavian social code, janteloven — the unwritten rule that no one should think themselves special, that boasting about size or success invites quiet correction. It shows up today in OpenAI’s own investors learning its revenue run-rate sits $20 billion below the figure that had been circulating, in TCS quietly beating estimates by 15% with none of the fanfare that number deserves, and in a stock market that spent Thursday getting corrected hard for having run ahead of itself.

AI & Tech

OpenAI discloses an annualised revenue of roughly $50 billion — $20 billion short of the figure investors had been citing — sending Nvidia, Oracle and CoreWeave shares lower, even as TSMC’s September revenue climbs 54.6% year-on-year and Microsoft unveils its Nvidia-powered Surface Laptop Ultra, while Apple reportedly lines up an October 13 event for a Siri-driven smart home hub and Google DeepMind says Gemini 4 could ship well before year-end

Takeaway OpenAI’s own revenue numbers turning out smaller than advertised wobbled the AI trade today, even as the chips and hardware underneath it — TSMC’s order book, Microsoft’s new AI laptop — keep running hot regardless of the hype gap.

OpenAI’s annualized revenue comes in at $50 billion — $20 billion below the figure investors had been citing

OpenAI told investors its annualized revenue stood at roughly $50 billion as of the end of September, the Financial Times reported on October 8 — about $20 billion below the roughly $70 billion figure that had circulated among investors in recent weeks. The gap traces partly to a methodology difference — rival Anthropic’s own revenue figures include sales through cloud partners such as AWS and Google Cloud, while OpenAI’s do not — but the disclosure still knocked shares of Nvidia, Oracle and CoreWeave lower on Thursday.

Takeaway A $20 billion gap between the number everyone was repeating and the number OpenAI actually gave its own investors is the kind of reality check that moves markets, methodology excuse or not.

TechCrunch →

TSMC’s September revenue climbs 54.6% year-on-year, signaling AI chip demand hasn’t cooled

Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. reported September 2026 revenue of NT$511.86 billion in a filing dated October 8 — down a slight 0.6% from August but up 54.6% from a year earlier. Revenue for January through September came in at NT$3.90 trillion, up 41.1% year-on-year, tracking above the high end of the company’s own third-quarter guidance.

Takeaway TSMC’s order book getting bigger the same week OpenAI’s numbers get scrutinized suggests the AI buildout itself isn’t what’s rattling investors — it’s whose revenue claims to trust.

TSMC (SEC filing) →

Microsoft unveils the Surface Laptop Ultra, its first laptop built around Nvidia’s RTX Spark AI chip

Microsoft used its October 7 Windows and Surface event in San Francisco — its first dedicated hardware showcase in more than two years — to formally introduce the Surface Laptop Ultra, built on Nvidia’s RTX Spark platform with up to 128GB of unified memory. Early hands-on reviews describe it as Microsoft’s most powerful Surface laptop yet, aimed squarely at local AI workloads rather than cloud-dependent ones.

Takeaway Building a flagship laptop around a chip platform rather than a CPU generation is Microsoft betting that “how much AI runs on your lap” becomes the next spec war.

Tom’s Guide →

Security researchers flag active exploitation attempts against already-patched Citrix NetScaler appliances

Researcher Kevin Beaumont reported exploitation attempts — which he has dubbed “PitScaler 2” — against NetScaler honeypots that had already received Citrix’s patch for CVE-2026-88779, a flaw Citrix describes as a denial-of-service issue. Some administrators reported patched appliances rebooting unexpectedly, and a script obtained by researchers attempts to plant persistent web shells and exfiltrate device configurations, though there is no confirmed case yet of it succeeding.

Takeaway Attackers going after boxes that were already patched is the reminder that “patched” and “safe” aren’t the same thing while a flaw’s full exploitability is still being worked out.

SecurityWeek →

Apple reportedly plans an October 13 event for a Siri-powered smart home hub, new HomePod mini and Apple TV

Bloomberg’s Mark Gurman reported Apple is planning an October 13 event to unveil three new devices built around Siri: a smart home hub with a roughly 6-inch display, an updated HomePod mini, and a refreshed Apple TV. Apple hasn’t confirmed the event or any of the products, and Gurman notes a larger HomePod update is still coming separately, later.

Takeaway Launching three Siri-first devices together, rather than one at a time, suggests Apple wants its “finally good at AI” story to land all at once.

MacRumors →

Google DeepMind says Gemini 4 is in post-training and could ship well before year-end

Google DeepMind’s Koray Kavukcuoglu said Gemini 4 has entered post-training and that the company wants to release it “much earlier” than the end of 2026, though he gave no firm date. The model is reportedly already being tested inside Google’s Antigravity coding tool, following a string of smaller Flash-tier releases since Gemini 3.5 shipped in May.

Takeaway Promising a release “much earlier” than a deadline that’s still ten weeks away is Google trying to get ahead of the Gemini 3.5 Pro delay story before it repeats.

Product & Business

TCS posts a 15% jump in quarterly profit and crosses $3 billion in annualised AI revenue as Flipkart’s Big Billion Days opens to the public today and Myntra, Cleartrip launch rival festive sales, even as Jio Platforms edges toward its final IPO papers, HCLTech sets October 12 for its own results, Ather Energy crosses 8 lakh cumulative sales to widen its lead over Ola Electric, and India Mobile Congress’s second day lines up keynotes from Akash Ambani, Sunil Mittal and Kumar Mangalam Birla

Takeaway Three of India’s biggest calendar events — TCS’s earnings, Flipkart’s festive sale and Jio’s IPO countdown — are all converging on the same week, with strong deal flow still the backdrop despite the RBI’s new caution.

TCS posts a 15% jump in quarterly profit and crosses $3 billion in annualised AI revenue

Tata Consultancy Services reported Q2 FY27 consolidated net profit of ₹13,884 crore, up 15% year-on-year, on revenue of ₹73,188 crore, up 11.22%, in results announced after market hours on October 8. The board declared a second interim dividend of ₹12 per share, with a record date of October 14, and said annualised revenue from AI work has crossed $3 billion while net cash from operations came in at ₹14,190 crore — 102.2% of net income.

Takeaway A clean double-digit beat landing the same day the broader market hit a 52-week low is TCS handing Dalal Street one genuine reason to look past Thursday’s rout.

Outlook Business →

Flipkart’s Big Billion Days opens to the public today, its 13th and biggest edition yet

Flipkart’s Big Billion Days — now in its 13th edition — opens to all shoppers today, October 9, after midnight access for Flipkart Plus and VIP members. Flipkart says this year’s sale carries its highest-ever number of participating sellers, with transacting sellers up more than 60% from last year, across over 150 million products.

Takeaway A 60% jump in participating sellers in one year says more about how many small businesses now depend on one sale window than it does about Flipkart’s own growth.

Myntra and Cleartrip launch rival festive sales alongside Flipkart’s Big Billion Days

Myntra and travel platform Cleartrip have both rolled out their own festive sale events timed to overlap with Flipkart’s Big Billion Days, as India’s biggest annual sale windows for fashion, travel and general retail land in the same week rather than spread across the month.

Takeaway Three big consumer platforms picking the same week rather than staggering their sales bets on shoppers having more festive budget to go around, not less competition for it.

Forrester forecasts Flipkart will cross $1 billion in sales during this year’s Big Billion Days

Forrester projects Flipkart’s Big Billion Days will cross $1 billion in gross sales this year, which would make it one of the largest single e-commerce sale events anywhere by that measure, as the platform leans on an expanded seller base and categories beyond its traditional electronics and fashion core.

Takeaway Hitting $1 billion in sales during a single festival window turns a seasonal discount event into a genuine macro indicator of festive-season demand.

Forrester →

Jio Platforms edges toward its final IPO papers, eyeing a listing before month-end

Jio Platforms has wrapped overseas investor roadshows across the US, UK, Dubai, Singapore and Hong Kong and is expected to file its red herring prospectus with SEBI in the week starting October 12, people familiar with the process told PTI. Banking sources say the issue — expected to raise around $3.8 billion at an enterprise valuation near $143-146 billion — could open after Dussehra and list by month-end, with proceeds earmarked largely to pay down roughly ₹27,500 crore of Reliance Jio Infocomm’s borrowings.

Takeaway Routing nearly all the IPO proceeds toward paying down existing debt rather than new expansion is a telling sign of what this listing is really designed to fix.

HCLTech sets October 12 for its own Q2 results and a third interim dividend

HCLTech’s board will meet on October 12 to approve its Q2 and first-half FY27 results and consider a third interim dividend for the year, the company said in an exchange filing, putting it third in line behind TCS and ahead of Infosys in this earnings season’s IT results calendar.

Takeaway Spacing out the big three IT results across two weeks means the market will be re-litigating “is the AI-disruption story overblown” three separate times this month.

Kotak Neo →

Ather Energy crosses 8 lakh cumulative sales and widens its lead over Ola Electric

Ather Energy crossed 8 lakh cumulative retail e-scooter sales on September 28, having delivered over 475,000 units since the start of 2025 alone, according to VAHAN portal data. Provisional September registration data puts Ather’s market share at roughly 14.7%, more than double Ola Electric’s 6.5% share, in a month where overall electric two-wheeler registrations rose nearly 90% year-on-year.

Takeaway Ather pulling more than double Ola’s market share in the same month total EV scooter sales nearly doubled shows this isn’t a shrinking category Ather is winning — it’s a growing one Ola is losing.

India Mobile Congress’s second day set to bring keynotes from Akash Ambani, Sunil Mittal and Kumar Mangalam Birla

The 10th India Mobile Congress, running through October 10 at Delhi’s Yashobhoomi under the theme “Scale Without Boundaries,” lines up its second day with keynotes expected from Reliance Jio chairman Akash Ambani, Bharti Enterprises founder Sunil Mittal and Aditya Birla Group chairman Kumar Mangalam Birla, alongside sessions spanning AI, 6G, quantum communications and satellite connectivity.

Takeaway Getting India’s three biggest telecom rivals on the same stage in the same week says the “scale without boundaries” theme is as much about the industry’s politics as its technology.

Sensex, Nifty tumble to a fresh 52-week low as a hawkish RBI and surging crude trigger a ₹10 lakh crore rout

The Sensex fell 1,045.46 points (1.44%) to close at 71,593.24 and the Nifty 50 dropped 371.25 points (1.64%) to 22,231.80 on October 8, with the Nifty touching a fresh 52-week intraday low of 22,179.90, as Wednesday’s hawkish RBI rate hike and crude pushing toward $105 a barrel combined to wipe out an estimated ₹10 lakh crore in investor wealth in a single session.

Takeaway A central bank turning hawkish and oil spiking in the same week is the kind of double shock that doesn’t need a third cause to explain a selloff this size.

Technosports →

Wall Street’s Nasdaq slides 1.3% as OpenAI’s revenue disclosure spooks the AI trade

The S&P 500 fell 0.47% to 7,765.36 and the Nasdaq Composite dropped 1.3% to 27,193.34 on October 8, its steepest one-day fall in weeks, after OpenAI’s disclosed revenue shortfall dragged Nvidia, Oracle and CoreWeave lower; the Dow Jones Industrial Average held nearly flat, up 0.1% to 51,231.64, as money rotated out of the most AI-exposed names.

Takeaway The Dow barely moving while the Nasdaq dropped 1.3% on the same day is the market drawing a clean line between “AI-exposed” stocks and everything else.

GIFT Nifty signals a modest rebound this morning as crude softens in Asian trade

GIFT Nifty futures were trading around 22,374-22,423 this morning, up roughly 80 points from Thursday’s close, as oil prices eased back in Asian trading and traders looked past Thursday’s rout for a steadier Friday open, even as IT stocks stay in focus heading into TCS’s post-results reaction and HCLTech’s own results due next week.

Takeaway A positive GIFT Nifty signal the morning after a ₹10 lakh crore wipeout says traders see Thursday as a one-day shock rather than the start of a deeper slide — for now.

Business Standard →

TCS’s 15% profit beat gives the market its first solid earnings read of the season

TCS’s after-hours numbers — a 15% rise in net profit, an 11.22% revenue gain and AI-linked annualised revenue crossing $3 billion — arrive as the market’s first real data point on whether IT earnings can offset September’s selloff, with HCLTech and Infosys still to report on October 12 and October 23 respectively.

Takeaway One earnings beat the morning after a 52-week low doesn’t erase a rout, but it does give today’s IT trade its first fundamental reason to stop falling.
