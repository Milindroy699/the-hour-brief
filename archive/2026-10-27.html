Before the news

There’s a Japanese art of repair called kintsugi — mending broken pottery with gold-dusted lacquer so the crack becomes part of the object’s history instead of a flaw to hide. It shows up today in Anthropic disclosing a security startup’s 12-hour breach of its own business systems on the very day it filed to go public; in the Sensex and Nifty’s broad rebound after four straight losing sessions; and in HDFC Bank completing a handover it telegraphed weeks ago, rather than papering over the gap between a board seat and the chief executive’s chair.

Anthropic publicly files for a Nasdaq IPO under the ticker ANT, with its S-1 disclosing an $80.7 billion first-half net loss against $16.3 billion in revenue, as a security startup discloses finding a path into Anthropic’s internal business systems in under 12 hours, even as Meta launches a $95 billion stock offering to fund its AI buildout, and Microsoft, Alphabet and Amazon head into this week’s earnings with Wall Street scrutinizing a combined $725 billion of 2026 AI capex

Takeaway Today’s AI news is about money and trust changing hands in public view — a lab opening its books to investors, a breach disclosed rather than buried, and three hyperscalers about to face their first earnings-season test of $725 billion in AI spending.

Anthropic publicly files for a Nasdaq IPO under the ticker ANT

Anthropic filed its S-1 registration with the SEC on October 25, seeking to list on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the ticker ANT, with Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley among the lead underwriters, according to NPR and the company’s own SEC filing. The filing leaves the price range, share count and valuation blank, though press estimates range from $1.5 trillion to $2 trillion; Anthropic’s last private round, in May, valued it at $965 billion.

Takeaway Filing before naming a price lets Anthropic test investor appetite against a $1.5-2 trillion range in public, rather than betting everything on a number fixed in private.

Anthropic’s S-1 discloses an $80.7 billion first-half net loss against $16.3 billion in revenue

Anthropic’s newly public S-1 shows first-half 2026 revenue of $16.3 billion against a net loss of $80.7 billion, net revenue retention near 900%, and $131.7 billion in cash and short-term investments, according to Yahoo Finance’s review of the filing. The filing also discloses more than $500 billion in long-term compute and infrastructure commitments, and says co-founders will retain 50.1% of the vote through a single Class F share held by a Founder LLC, with the Long-Term Benefit Trust electing a majority of the board.

Takeaway Keeping $131.7 billion in cash against an $80.7 billion loss is Anthropic telling investors it can absorb years of losses like this one without needing the IPO money itself to survive.

A security startup says its AI agents reached Anthropic’s internal business systems in under 12 hours

Cathedral, a cybersecurity startup founded by four former DOGE staffers, said its autonomous AI agents found a path into a limited set of Anthropic’s sales, HR and IT systems within 12 hours, working under Anthropic’s own outside-researcher program, the Washington Post reported October 26. Anthropic said the entry point was a flaw in third-party administrative software rather than Claude itself, that customer prompts, model weights and source code were never exposed, and that it disabled the affected software the same day; Cathedral said it reached meeting transcripts, salary data and Slack messages, and that Anthropic understated how serious the access was.

Takeaway Disclosing an authorized red team’s 12-hour breach on the same day as its IPO filing is Anthropic betting that transparency reads better to investors than a cover-up would.

Meta launches a $95 billion stock offering to fund its AI buildout

Meta said October 26 it plans to raise up to $95 billion in equity for AI infrastructure and “superintelligence” work, starting with $35 billion in underwritten public offerings — about $22.5 billion in Class A common stock and $12.5 billion in preferred depositary shares — before raising up to $60 billion more through an at-the-market program, according to CNBC and Bloomberg. Nvidia, AMD and Broadcom together indicated interest in buying up to $5 billion of the shares; Meta’s stock fell 1.6% in morning trading, and the company still projects $135-145 billion in 2026 capital spending.

Takeaway Three of Meta’s own chip suppliers each buying a slice of its stock offering shows its AI bet now runs on financing arrangements that tie supplier and customer together.

Microsoft and Alphabet report Wednesday, Amazon Thursday, as Wall Street tests $725 billion of AI capex

Microsoft and Alphabet are both scheduled to report quarterly earnings after the close Wednesday, October 28, with Amazon following Thursday, according to a Benzinga preview published October 26. Combined 2026 AI capital-spending guidance across the four largest hyperscalers has climbed to roughly $725 billion, up from about $670 billion heading into the prior earnings cycle, with Alphabet alone guiding to $195-205 billion for the year.

Takeaway Three hyperscalers reporting in the same 48 hours turns this week into a single concentrated verdict on whether $725 billion of AI spending is translating into revenue.

Product & Business

Anup Bagchi begins his RBI-approved three-year term as HDFC Bank’s MD & CEO today as DoorDash reportedly moves into advanced talks to acquire Instacart for about $17 billion, even as EQT agrees to acquire Range Resources in an all-stock deal valuing the combined gas producer above $50 billion, Reddit’s own UX team built an internal AI prototyping tool now used by its entire product organization, and Verizon raises its full-year guidance for a third straight quarter alongside a $2 billion AI infrastructure deal with AWS

Takeaway Succession and consolidation are today’s throughline — a bank completing a handover it telegraphed weeks ago, two food-delivery rivals edging toward a combination, and an energy merger and a product team’s internal tool both betting that building scale in-house beats building it alone.

Anup Bagchi begins his three-year term as HDFC Bank’s MD & CEO today

Anup Bagchi’s RBI-approved three-year term as HDFC Bank’s MD & CEO began today, October 27, a day after Sashidhar Jagdishan’s tenure ended; Bagchi joined the bank’s board as an additional director on October 2 and was previously MD & CEO of ICICI Prudential Life Insurance, according to Business Today. Analysts expect his early focus to fall on deposit growth, loan expansion and net interest margins rather than a strategic overhaul.

Takeaway Actually taking the chair today, after three weeks already on the board, is India’s largest private bank finishing the quietest possible handover it started weeks ago.

DoorDash is reportedly in advanced talks to acquire Instacart for about $17 billion

DoorDash is in advanced talks to acquire Instacart in a deal valued at roughly $17 billion that could be announced within days, the Wall Street Journal reported October 26, with Reuters citing people familiar saying an agreement could be finalized as early as this week. Instacart shares jumped 7.9% Monday, extending a 9% gain from Friday after an earlier report the company was exploring a sale; DoorDash shares fell 5% on the news, and the report says DoorDash’s separate talks with Uber are no longer active.

Takeaway DoorDash and Instacart held this same conversation in 2021 and walked away over antitrust worries — trying again now is a bet that the math looks different this time.

EQT agrees to acquire Range Resources in an all-stock deal valuing the combined company above $50 billion

EQT and Range Resources signed a definitive all-stock merger agreement on October 26 that the companies describe as creating America’s largest vertically integrated natural gas producer, with a combined pro forma enterprise value above $50 billion, according to the companies’ SEC filings. The two project roughly $20 billion in cumulative free cash flow over the next five years at current strip pricing and expect investment-grade credit metrics at closing.

Takeaway Combining a driller with pipeline and processing assets in one all-stock deal is a bet that owning the whole gas supply chain is worth more than owning any single piece of it.

Lenny’s Newsletter: why Reddit built its own internal AI prototyping tool

Reddit principal UX engineer Blair Metcalf built an internal platform called Proto that lets designers and product managers turn ideas into production-like prototypes, using a design-system MCP that maps Reddit’s product language to production code, according to an October 26 Lenny’s Newsletter post. Proto has reached 100% adoption across Reddit’s UX and product organization, with more than 700 prototypes now live on the platform, and lets testers sign in with real Reddit accounts and send annotated feedback straight to Cursor for targeted edits.

Takeaway A prototyping tool hitting 100% internal adoption in a product org says more about how fast AI-assisted prototyping is normalizing than any single flashy demo would.

Verizon raises full-year guidance for a third straight quarter, announces $2 billion AI deal with AWS

Verizon raised its full-year adjusted EPS guidance to $5.04-$5.06, from $4.99-$5.04, after beating Q3 estimates, marking the company’s third consecutive quarterly raise, according to its October 26 earnings release. Alongside the results, Verizon disclosed a more than $2 billion AI infrastructure agreement with AWS to build high-capacity fiber routes connecting AWS data centers.

Takeaway A phone company’s fiber contract with a cloud provider is itself an AI-capex story now — it just shows up in a telecom’s earnings release instead of a hyperscaler’s.

Sensex and Nifty snap a four-session losing streak with a broad Monday rebound

The Sensex closed 482.92 points (0.66%) higher at 73,606.75 and the Nifty 50 gained 153.20 points (0.68%) to settle at 22,753.20 on Monday, October 26, snapping a four-session losing streak, according to India TV News and Business Standard. Metal, pharma and energy stocks led the rebound, with Adani Ports, Eternal, Kotak Mahindra Bank, Trent and Tata Steel among the Sensex’s biggest gainers, while Bajaj Finance, HDFC Bank and Maruti Suzuki weighed on the index.

Takeaway A rebound led by metal and pharma rather than the banks that dragged the index down all last week suggests this bounce is sector-rotation relief, not a broad change of mind.

Wall Street’s Dow, S&P 500 and Nasdaq all rise Monday as Anthropic’s IPO filing reignites the AI trade

All three main US indexes closed higher Monday, October 26, as investors parsed Anthropic’s newly public IPO filing and crude prices eased, with the Nasdaq Composite up 0.82% to 26,169.38, the S&P 500 up about 0.7% to 7,676.77, and the Dow Jones Industrial Average up 146.42 points (0.28%) to 52,361.58, according to Yahoo Finance. The gains followed Friday’s 652.82-point Dow slide and back-to-back weekly losses for the S&P 500 and Nasdaq.

Takeaway Wall Street rallying on an AI company’s own IPO paperwork, rather than on any new product or earnings beat, shows how much the broader market’s mood now rides on the AI trade specifically.

Jio Platforms’ shares are credited to demat accounts today, a day ahead of Wednesday’s listing

Jio Platforms is crediting shares from its record ₹30,213 crore IPO to successful applicants’ demat accounts today, October 27, a day after Monday’s basis of allotment was finalized, with a tentative listing on the NSE and BSE set for tomorrow, October 28, according to Chittorgarh. The issue closed at a record subscription of 22.19 times, and the grey-market premium has held near ₹243-250, implying a roughly 22% premium over the ₹1,119 upper price band.

Takeaway Shares actually landing in investors’ accounts today is the moment India’s largest-ever IPO stops being a subscription number and becomes something people can finally sell or hold.

The Fed’s two-day policy meeting begins today, with Wednesday’s decision expected to hold rates steady

The Federal Reserve’s Open Market Committee begins its two-day October meeting today, October 27, with a decision due tomorrow at 2 PM ET under Fed Chair Kevin Warsh. Markets are pricing roughly an 86% probability the Fed holds its benchmark rate at 3.75%-4.00% after September’s quarter-point hike, though the committee’s own September minutes had flagged a possible further increase by year-end.

Takeaway Markets betting heavily on a hold, even after the Fed’s own minutes left a hike on the table, shows investors reading Monday’s rally as confidence the central bank blinks first.
