Delete your account

You can delete your The Hour Brief account and everything it keeps at any time, in the app or by email. Using The Hour Brief never needs an account.

Last updated: 26 November 2026

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open The Hour Brief on your iPhone or Android phone.
  2. Tap your picture at the top right to open You.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account, then confirm.

Your account is removed from our servers at once.

Without the app: by email

milindroy101292@gmail.com

Write from any address with the subject “Delete my account” and the mobile number you signed in with (or say you used Google or Apple, and from which email). We delete the account within 7 days and confirm by reply. We may ask you to confirm the request from the same number or account first.

What is deleted

  • DeletedYour account and sign-in details (the keyed fingerprint of your number, your PIN’s hash, or your Google or Apple sign-in ID), and everything the account keeps: quiz results, streaks and badges, saved stories, your name, your section, text size and theme choices, and your league membership link.
  • KeptNothing tied to you. Anonymous counts (how many people opened a screen on a given day, quiz score totals) never had your identity attached. A league you played in keeps its daily scores under its generated player names until the league expires, about 120 days after its last activity.
  • On your phoneWhat the app stores on your phone stays there until you remove the app or clear its data.

See the privacy policy for everything an account keeps.
