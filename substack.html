Substack drafts

This week’s posts, ready to paste into Substack. Copy the title, the subtitle and the post, add the cover, publish.

  1. On Substack, start a new post (the button below opens the editor once your address is filled in).
  2. Copy title → paste in the title, Copy subtitle → paste in the subtitle.
  3. Copy post → click into the body and paste: headings, links and lists come along.
  4. Cover image → download it and add it in the post’s settings as the social preview image.
  5. Publish, send to email, and share it to Notes. One post a day at most; Sunday’s recap is the one to never miss.
