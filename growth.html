  • Visitors: browsers that opened the website that day (each counted once a day); not the apps. New: first day this browser ever visited.
  • Source: the first page of a visit decides it: a tagged link (?src= / utm_source, e.g. podcast, telegram, linkedin-page), else the site that sent them (google, chatgpt, linkedin…), else direct (typed, bookmarks, and apps like WhatsApp that hide where a link was opened).
  • Store taps: taps on any "Get the app" link (the bar, footer badges, end of the brief, quiz, Audio, share pages, podcast and post links). Installs: a phone opening the app for the first time.
  • Installs can't be tied to one visit here. For installs by campaign: Google Play Console › Grow users › Acquisition (from the utm tags), and App Store Connect › App Analytics › Acquisition › Campaigns (once the provider token is set in app.html).
  • Sources and places are recorded from 21 Nov 2026; earlier days show totals only.
