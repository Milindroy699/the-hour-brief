  • Visitors: browsers that opened the website that day (each counted once a day); not the apps. New: first day this browser ever visited.
  • Source: the first page of a visit decides it: a tagged link (?src= / utm_source, e.g. podcast, telegram, linkedin-page), else the site that sent them (google, chatgpt, linkedin…), else direct (typed, bookmarks, and apps like WhatsApp that hide where a link was opened).
  • Store taps: taps on any "Get the app" link (the bar, footer badges, end of the brief, quiz, Audio, share pages, podcast and post links). Installs: a phone opening the app for the first time.
  • Installs can't be tied to one visit here. For installs by campaign: Google Play Console › Grow users › Acquisition (from the utm tags), and App Store Connect › App Analytics › Acquisition › Campaigns (once the provider token is set in app.html).
  • Sources and places are recorded from 21 Nov 2026; earlier days show totals only.

Sundays

  • Weekly recap (“What actually mattered this week”) is written and posted to your LinkedIn profile on its own. Its X post comes on Telegram with a button.
  • Reddit: the recap’s Reddit draft comes on Telegram. One community a week, read its rules first, stay for comments in the first hour.
  • Review (10 min): X plan → replies per day vs target, followers trend, visits and app taps from X. Analytics → which sources bring visitors and app taps. Do more of what moved; if X visits stay flat for two weeks while replies go up, change topics, not volume.

When something shows ✕

  • Edition / audio / podcast late: the morning pipeline is stuck. Tell Claude; usually a failed run or an empty Sarvam (voice) balance.
  • Post kit or X pack missing: the “Daily post writer” routine didn’t run or failed. Telegram, LinkedIn and X posts wait for it; tell Claude.
  • YouTube Short / Instagram Reel missing: the video was made but not posted. Usually an expired Instagram token or a YouTube sign-in; Connections turns red too.
  • LinkedIn profile: the connection lasts 60 days. Reconnect before the date in Connections (a reminder comes on Telegram a week before).
  • Telegram messages missing: the bot lost its chat. Message the bot, then ask Claude to re-pair it.
  • A league name reported (Telegram 🚩): look within a day; tap the link to replace it if it’s offensive.
